---- What it is ----
Watch 47 turns the C47 into the classic three-button digital watch: the time, the date and the seconds from the
calculator's clock, 12 h with AM / PM or 24 h, the day of the week, a daily alarm that really beeps, an hourly
chime, a stopwatch with split, the time setting and the light (here a pixel dissolve). Its three buttons are the
C47 keys 7, 4 and 9. The face is an original drawing in the spirit of the watch, not a copy of any brand's.

---- How to use it ----
Load WATCH47 and press XEQ WATCH47. The button you press is shown pressed on the screen.
  7  LIGHT      time: the light for 1.5 s; alarm: set it; stopwatch: split / reset; setting: the next field
  4  MODE       time -> alarm (AL) -> stopwatch (ST) -> time setting -> time
  9  the right  time: 12 / 24 h; alarm: ALM -> ALM SIG -> SIG -> none (alarm and hourly chime on or off);
     button     stopwatch: start / stop; setting: + 1
  0             the end
Setting the alarm: in AL press 7 (the hour flashes), 9 adds one, 7 the minutes, 9 adds one, 4 ends (the alarm is
on after a setting). At its minute the watch beeps for 20 s; any button stops it. With SIG on it beeps on the hour.
Setting the time: the seconds flash, 9 puts them to 00; 7 moves on to the hour, the minutes, the month, the day,
9 adds one; 4 back to the time. The day of the week follows from the date.
The stopwatch counts tenths (the C47's TICKS) and keeps running in the other modes. Easter egg: press 9 twice
quickly in the time mode.

---- What it changes on your calculator ----
Your stack and LASTx are given back at the end. Registers R00-R19 are not touched; R20-R99 are used and cleared at
the start and the end. Two variables stay between runs: WSET (12 / 24 h, the alarm, the chime, the alarm time) and
WOFF (the watch's difference from the calculator's clock, in days; 0 STO "WOFF" goes back to the calculator's
time). The screen is cleared at the end; GRMOD 0 and GRFNT 20 are set; WSIZE is set to 64. A running program
keeps the calculator awake: leave with 0 when you are done.

---- Programs and labels ----
One program, WATCH47, with one global label (WATCH47). Every routine inside is a local label (LBL 00-37 the
digits, letters and indicators, called with XEQ IND, and the named routines), so nothing else is added to your
program catalogue. It uses the STRUCT commands, ATEXT, GRFNT, AGRAPH (with GRMOD 4 for the lit display) and BUZZ;
GRMOD 4 with AGRAPH and BUZZ inside a program have not yet been run on a calculator (see the README).
