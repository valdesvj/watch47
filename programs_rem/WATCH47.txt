REM WATCH47 v0.1.0 - Watch 47: a tribute to the classic three-button digital watch, on the C47 / R47
REM The time, the date and the seconds from the calculator's clock (plus an offset set on the watch), 12 h
REM with AM / PM or 24 h; the daily alarm (it beeps 20 s: BUZZ), the hourly chime, the stopwatch (1/10 s:
REM TICKS), the time setting; the light (a pixel dissolve); the Easter egg: 9 twice in the time mode shows C47.
REM The three buttons are the C47 keys 7 (LIGHT), 4 (MODE) and 9 (ALARM ON-OFF / 24H / START-STOP); 0 ends.
REM Modes (4 each time): time -> alarm (AL) -> stopwatch (ST) -> time setting -> time.
REM   time:      7 the light (1.5 s), 9 12 h / 24 h (twice quickly: C47 for 2 s, and the setting as it was)
REM   alarm:     9 alarm and chime: ALM -> ALM SIG -> SIG -> none; 7 set: the hour flashes, 9 + 1, 7 the
REM              minutes, 7 the hour again ...; 4 ends the setting (the alarm is on after a setting)
REM   stopwatch: 9 start / stop; 7 running: split (SPL) and back, stopped: reset
REM   setting:   7 the next field: seconds -> hour -> minutes -> month -> day; 9: seconds to 00 (from 30: the
REM              next minute), the others + 1; the day of the week from the date
REM The settings are kept between runs in the global variables WSET (24 h, alarm, chime, the alarm time)
REM and WOFF (the watch's offset from the calculator's clock, in days; 0 STO "WOFF" goes back to the clock).
REM Screen 400 x 240, row 0 = bottom. The LCD's inside (x 93-306, rows 37-186) is drawn with AGRAPH words in
REM bands of 64 rows from rows 37 and 101 (GRMOD 1: written as they are; lit: GRMOD 4, the inverse), so a
REM cell needs no clearing and the light is the same drawing inverted. The light on or off: four XOR passes of
REM a 4 x 4 ordered dither over the inside (the pixel effect, LBL :DISS:), then the flag R22.
REM Cells (targets R50-R60, shown R61-R71): 0 1 the hour (or the stopwatch's minutes), 2 3 the minutes (its
REM seconds), 4 5 the small digits (the seconds, its 1/10 s), 6 7 the date (the month when it is set),
REM 8 the colon, 9 the letters (0-6 SU-SA, 7 AL, 8 ST), 10 the indicators (1 AM 2 PM 4 24H 8 ALM 16 SIG
REM 32 SPL). A cell is drawn only when it changes. Codes of a digit: 0-9, 10 blank, 11 the C of C47.
REM Labels: LBL 00-11 the big digits, LBL 12-22 the small ones (their AGRAPH words in R80-R82), LBL 23-31 the
REM letters (column words, LBL :COL:), LBL 32-37 the indicators.
REM Registers: R20 the mode (0 time, 1 alarm, 2 stopwatch, 3 setting), R21 the field (alarm 0 none 1 hour
REM 2 minutes; setting 0 seconds 1 hour 2 minutes 3 month 4 day), R22 1 lit, R23 the TICKS the light ends,
REM R24 1 24 h, R25 R26 the alarm's hour and minutes, R27 the alarm on, R28 the chime on, R29 the offset
REM (days), R30 the stopwatch running, R31 its time before the start (1/10 s), R32 the TICKS of the start,
REM R33 1 split shown, R34 the key, R35 the key before, R36 its TICKS, R37 the TICKS the alarm stops (0 none),
REM R38 the TICKS the C47 ends (0 none), R39 the watch's seconds (from the JD's day 0), R40-R42 year month
REM day, R43-R45 hours minutes seconds, R46 the weekday (0 Sunday), R47 the day number, R48 the minute
REM number (a new minute: the alarm, the chime), R49 the flashing phase (TICKS / 5), R72 the split time,
REM R73 a counter, R74 the code drawn, R75-R79 work, R80-R82 a glyph's words (the dither words R80-R83),
REM R83 the scale (2^rows above the band), R84 the AGRAPH word, R85 x, R86 the band's row, R87 1 / 0 an
REM indicator on / off, R88 the glyph labels' first, R89 R90 the side and middle columns of a digit, R92 R93
REM the button shown inverted (x, row; 0 none), R94 23 ones, R95 the second of the last alarm beep,
REM R96 TICKS now, R97 the cell, R98 the target of LBL :D2Z:, R99 work. R20-R99 cleared at the end.
REM The user's stack and LASTx are kept (LocR 9: STOS R.00, LASTx in R.08).
REM Control flow in the C47 STRUCT commands (IF / ELSE / ENDIF, DO / WHILE / ENDDO), indented two spaces
