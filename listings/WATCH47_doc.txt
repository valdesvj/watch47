      ; WATCH47 v0.1.0 - Watch 47: a tribute to the classic three-button digital watch, on the C47 / R47
      ; The time, the date and the seconds from the calculator's clock (plus an offset set on the watch), 12 h
      ; with AM / PM or 24 h; the daily alarm (it beeps 20 s: BUZZ), the hourly chime, the stopwatch (1/10 s:
      ; TICKS), the time setting; the light (a pixel dissolve); the Easter egg: 9 twice in the time mode shows C47.
      ; The three buttons are the C47 keys 7 (LIGHT), 4 (MODE) and 9 (ALARM ON-OFF / 24H / START-STOP); 0 ends.
      ; Modes (4 each time): time -> alarm (AL) -> stopwatch (ST) -> time setting -> time.
      ; time:      7 the light (1.5 s), 9 12 h / 24 h (twice quickly: C47 for 2 s, and the setting as it was)
      ; alarm:     9 alarm and chime: ALM -> ALM SIG -> SIG -> none; 7 set: the hour flashes, 9 + 1, 7 the
      ; minutes, 7 the hour again ...; 4 ends the setting (the alarm is on after a setting)
      ; stopwatch: 9 start / stop; 7 running: split (SPL) and back, stopped: reset
      ; setting:   7 the next field: seconds -> hour -> minutes -> month -> day; 9: seconds to 00 (from 30: the
      ; next minute), the others + 1; the day of the week from the date
      ; The settings are kept between runs in the global variables WSET (24 h, alarm, chime, the alarm time)
      ; and WOFF (the watch's offset from the calculator's clock, in days; 0 STO "WOFF" goes back to the clock).
      ; Screen 400 x 240, row 0 = bottom. The LCD's inside (x 93-306, rows 37-186) is drawn with AGRAPH words in
      ; bands of 64 rows from rows 37 and 101 (GRMOD 1: written as they are; lit: GRMOD 4, the inverse), so a
      ; cell needs no clearing and the light is the same drawing inverted. The light on or off: four XOR passes of
      ; a 4 x 4 ordered dither over the inside (the pixel effect, LBL :DISS:), then the flag R22.
      ; Cells (targets R50-R60, shown R61-R71): 0 1 the hour (or the stopwatch's minutes), 2 3 the minutes (its
      ; seconds), 4 5 the small digits (the seconds, its 1/10 s), 6 7 the date (the month when it is set),
      ; 8 the colon, 9 the letters (0-6 SU-SA, 7 AL, 8 ST), 10 the indicators (1 AM 2 PM 4 24H 8 ALM 16 SIG
      ; 32 SPL). A cell is drawn only when it changes. Codes of a digit: 0-9, 10 blank, 11 the C of C47.
      ; Labels: LBL 00-11 the big digits, LBL 12-22 the small ones (their AGRAPH words in R80-R82), LBL 23-31 the
      ; letters (column words, LBL :COL:), LBL 32-37 the indicators.
      ; Registers: R20 the mode (0 time, 1 alarm, 2 stopwatch, 3 setting), R21 the field (alarm 0 none 1 hour
      ; 2 minutes; setting 0 seconds 1 hour 2 minutes 3 month 4 day), R22 1 lit, R23 the TICKS the light ends,
      ; R24 1 24 h, R25 R26 the alarm's hour and minutes, R27 the alarm on, R28 the chime on, R29 the offset
      ; (days), R30 the stopwatch running, R31 its time before the start (1/10 s), R32 the TICKS of the start,
      ; R33 1 split shown, R34 the key, R35 the key before, R36 its TICKS, R37 the TICKS the alarm stops (0 none),
      ; R38 the TICKS the C47 ends (0 none), R39 the watch's seconds (from the JD's day 0), R40-R42 year month
      ; day, R43-R45 hours minutes seconds, R46 the weekday (0 Sunday), R47 the day number, R48 the minute
      ; number (a new minute: the alarm, the chime), R49 the flashing phase (TICKS / 5), R72 the split time,
      ; R73 a counter, R74 the code drawn, R75-R79 work, R80-R82 a glyph's words (the dither words R80-R83),
      ; R83 the scale (2^rows above the band), R84 the AGRAPH word, R85 x, R86 the band's row, R87 1 / 0 an
      ; indicator on / off, R88 the glyph labels' first, R89 R90 the side and middle columns of a digit, R92 R93
      ; the button shown inverted (x, row; 0 none), R94 23 ones, R95 the second of the last alarm beep,
      ; R96 TICKS now, R97 the cell, R98 the target of LBL :D2Z:, R99 work. R20-R99 cleared at the end.
      ; The user's stack and LASTx are kept (LocR 9: STOS R.00, LASTx in R.08).
      ; Control flow in the C47 STRUCT commands (IF / ELSE / ENDIF, DO / WHILE / ENDDO), indented two spaces
      ; per level; tools/build_watch47.py checks them, numbers them and fills the REM ==== lines and {NAME}s.

   2  LocR 9

   4  LASTx

   6  XEQ :INIT:    (93)
